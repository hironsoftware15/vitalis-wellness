Privacy Policy for NexVital: AI Longevity Coach

Effective Date: October 19, 2026 • App: NexVital (com.vitalis.wellness)

1. Overview & Commitment

NexVital ("we", "our") is a consumer health and longevity companion engineered to empower individuals with personalized preventive fitness planning, circadian nutrition, sleep optimization, medication adherence, and habit tracking. Your biometric telemetry is confidential, encrypted, and protected in accordance with the Apple HealthKit Privacy Guidelines, Google Play Health Data Policy, and modern privacy standards.

2. Google Health Connect Data Collection & Use

NexVital requests access to Google Health Connect APIs for the following specific purposes:

  • Steps & Active Calories: To evaluate daily movement volume and calibrate Zone-2 aerobic cardio targets.
  • Sleep Sessions (Deep, REM, Light Stages): To evaluate physiological recovery readiness and calculate circadian caffeine cutoffs.
  • Heart Rate & Resting Heart Rate: To assess cardiovascular recovery, autonomic balance, and physiological strain.

Strict Prohibition on Data Sale: We NEVER transfer, sell, license, or disclose your Health Connect biometrics to third-party data brokers, advertising networks, or data aggregators. Health Connect data is used solely to provide user-facing wellness coaching features.

3. AI Processing & Privacy Guardrails

Conversational longevity coaching and personalized meal recommendations are processed via Google Gemini 3.8 Flash with strict privacy isolation. Your personal health telemetry is never used to train generalized foundation models.

4. Data Security & Encryption

All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and encrypted at rest using AES-256 in Google Cloud Firestore.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated telemetry at any time directly through the app settings or by contacting privacy@medicore-wellness.web.app.
